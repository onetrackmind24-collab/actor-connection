Reference guide

Scoring and eligible credits

Use this guide when you want the details behind your score or an eligible connection.

Eligible movie connections

Both actors must appear in the same movie. TV roles, crew credits, cameos, and appearances limited to end-credit scenes do not count. Voice roles and an actor or performer playing themselves in a movie count.

The Cut and bonuses

The Cut stays hidden until the round ends. Finish in fewer connections than the Cut to earn a Quick Cut bonus of 25 points for each connection under it. Independently discovered obscure credits can earn Deep Cut points, up to 25 points per round.

Hints

  • Hint 1 reveals a movie and costs 10 points. Using Hint 2 reveals a connecting actor and raises the round’s total hint penalty to 20 points.
  • A movie revealed by a hint earns no Deep Cut points or bonus backtracks, even if you backtrack and use it again.
  • Finishing in six connections with hints scores −10 or −20 before any paid backtracks. Bonuses cannot offset that penalty.
  • In a no-hints round, hints remain disabled for the whole attempt.

Backtracking

Your first backtrack is free. Each 10 Deep Cut points earns another free backtrack. Additional backtracks cost 5 points each. Backtracking removes your latest connection; hints already used still count.

Challenges

Custom actor pairs are available in Challenge Mode. A movie route within six connections must be verified before a link is created. Each invitation fixes the actors and hint setting for seven days. The solution stays hidden until the round ends.

A challenge created from a finished round also carries a score to beat. Reaching the target beats an unfinished attempt; between completed attempts, the higher score wins.
