How to play

Connect the start actor to the target.

Build a chain of movie connections in six degrees or fewer. Every connection must use a movie both actors actually appeared in.

1Start with the actor shown. Pick a movie they appeared in.
2Choose a connecting actor. That actor must also appear in the same movie.
3Keep going. Your connecting actor becomes the new current actor.
4Reach the target in 6 or fewer. Six is still a win.
Movies only. No TV roles.
Voice roles count. An actor or performer playing themselves in a movie counts.
Hints cost points. Hint 1 gives the movie; Hint 2 gives the connecting actor. Finishing in six connections with hints scores −10 or −20 before paid backtracks; bonuses cannot offset this penalty.
Backtracking is allowed. Your first backtrack is free; additional free backs can be earned through Deep Cuts.
The Cut stays hidden. Beat it for a Quick Cut bonus. Obscure connections can earn separate Deep Cut points.
